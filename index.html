Jensen Huang resigns as Nvidia CEO, effective immediately

Nvidia announced Thursday that Jensen Huang — who co-founded the chipmaker 33 years ago and built it into the engine of the AI boom — has resigned as president, chief executive, and board member, effective immediately. CFO Colette Kress takes over as interim CEO while the board searches for a permanent successor, and director Harvey C. Jones becomes the board’s first-ever chair. The exit caps a week of turmoil: the board opened an investigation on Sunday into reports that Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence operation, then met Wednesday night after a report of a second affair. Nvidia shares fell about 12% in premarket trading. Third-quarter earnings are still scheduled for Tuesday, November 17.
