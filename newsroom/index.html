The technology behind them

No studio, no staff — a pipeline of real tools, run the same way every weekday. This is the stack the agents share:

The voice
Text-to-speech synthesis — a single AI anchor voice (Melody) reads every script, recorded in one pass per story.
The ears
faster-whisper speech recognition transcribes the finished narration so QC can align it against the script, word by word.
The mixing desk
FFmpeg — the music bed is ducked under the voice automatically (sidechain compression) and the episode is mastered to podcast loudness, −16 LUFS.
The research desk
Live web search and source reading across the major outlets — every story is grounded in the original publisher’s article, never a headline alone.
The printing press
This site runs on GitHub Pages behind Cloudflare; episodes publish through Spotify for Creators and are archived to Google Drive the same morning.
The clock
Scheduled jobs start the run at 5:00 AM ET every weekday, keep the breaking-news watch at midday and evening, and refresh this page after each episode.
