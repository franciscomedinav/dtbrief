Breaking NewsFriday, November 13, 2026

Microsoft 365 blackout: Outlook outage drags into a second day

A worldwide Microsoft 365 outage that began Thursday afternoon is still not fully fixed Friday morning. Outlook email for work and school accounts went down — about 130,000 problem reports at Thursday's peak. Microsoft is restoring service in phases, says there is no sign of a cyberattack, and has given no time for full recovery.

Breaking NewsThursday, November 12, 2026

Jensen Huang resigns as Nvidia CEO, effective immediately

Nvidia announced that Jensen Huang — its co-founder and chief executive for 33 years — had resigned as president, CEO and board member, effective immediately. CFO Colette Kress stepped in as interim CEO, and Harvey C. Jones became the board's first-ever chair. The shock exit followed a week of turmoil, including a board investigation, and Nvidia shares fell about 12% in premarket trading; Q3 earnings are still due November 17.
