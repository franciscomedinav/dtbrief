Contact

One inbox, three routes. Pick the one that fits and we’ll take it from there.

Daily Tech Briefing We reply within a couple of weekdays

The inbox

Everything goes to one place: info@dtbrief.com. Put the route in your subject line — “Tip”, “Correction” or “Business” — and it lands with the right priority.

News tips

See something we should cover? Send the link and one line about why it matters. We read every tip, though we can’t reply to all of them or promise coverage.

Corrections

If we got something wrong, we want to know. Corrections are taken seriously: we check the claim against the original source, fix the page in place, and add a note saying what changed. The faster you flag it, the faster it’s fixed.

Business & advertising

Sponsorships, partnerships and everything else business: start with the Advertise page, then write to the same inbox.

One honest note

This is a small, human-directed newsroom with AI help, not a call center. Replies usually land within a couple of weekdays.
